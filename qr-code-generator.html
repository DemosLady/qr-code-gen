What is a QR Code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a 2D barcode that stores data like URLs, text, WiFi credentials, or contact info. Any smartphone camera can scan it instantly. Created in 1994 by Denso Wave for the automotive industry, QR codes are now everywhere — menus, tickets, payments, and marketing.

How to Use This Generator

Tips for Better QR Codes

Use high contrast (dark foreground on light background). Keep URLs short. Test with multiple phones before printing. For print, use SVG format — it scales to any size without losing quality. Minimum recommended print size: 2×2 cm.
