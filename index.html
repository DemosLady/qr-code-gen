What is a QR Code?

A QR code is a 2D barcode invented in 1994. Scan with any phone camera to open URLs, connect to WiFi, or read text. Used everywhere — menus, tickets, payments, marketing.

Tips for Better QR Codes

Use high contrast (dark on light). Keep URLs short. Test before printing. Use SVG for print — scales perfectly. Minimum print size: 2×2 cm. All data stays in your browser.
